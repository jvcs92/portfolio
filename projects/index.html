  2. Flamin' Run: a 3D game that turns players into buyers

    Personal project · browser game for the shop · designed by me, generated with AI-assisted development

    The idea

    A small shop has no ad budget. So instead of paying for attention, I made something people want to play and share, with the shop built into it. I designed the game around the store and generated the code with AI; the growth mechanics, the links to the shop and the gamification are my part.

    How it works

    • A 3D runner on the phone. Six levels, six bosses. Swipe to change lane, jump or slide; grab the flame orb and burn the boss at the end of each level.
    • Rewards that lead to the shop. Beat the first boss and you unlock a real 15% discount code for the shop.
    • Outfits from the real catalogue. The character wears the shop's actual products, each with a "get the real one" link.
    • Built to spread. Players share their run on WhatsApp, X, Facebook or Telegram, and progress is saved on their phone so they come back.

    Growth loop

    • Play → win a discount → shop → share → new players
    • Every screen points back to the shop
    • Three.js (3D)
    • Gamification
    • Referral loop
    • Mobile-first
